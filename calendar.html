Talks & workshops.

A real woman from Warangal who built a career from home, and who can teach your audience to find their voice. Each talk runs as a 20, 45 or 90 minute session or workshop.

01

Rooted and Rising: Building a Career From Home

Your home, your town and your values are not your limits. A real career can start from exactly where you are.

Best for girls' colleges, women's clubs and community groups.

02

The Articulation Gap: Say What You Know So People Follow

You are smarter than your words. Learn to put what you know into clear, simple sentences people remember. It is about clarity, not accent.

Best for universities, MBA and placement cells.

03

Your Name Is a Brand: Personal Branding for Young Women

Before your first job, let your name stand for something, online and offline, while keeping your values and your privacy.

Best for final-year students and management departments.
